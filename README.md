# rocky-raccoon
Team Rocky Raccoon's project for HackGlobal

Our project is a website that can provide supplementary instruction for young students aged Preschool to Middle School, maybe even older. The goal of our project is to teach students about the proper safety measures to take in the case of emergency events such as earthquakes, fires or fire drills, and shelter-in-place.

Rocky Raccoon is our mascot, helping students feel comfortable and entertained by a cute character to chat with.
